# Live competitor and reference website audit

**Observed:** 7 October 2026
**Purpose:** Give future design and coding agents a dated, evidence-based reference register.
**Scope:** Public homepage content and structure returned by the browser. Mobile behavior, hidden states, conversion performance and analytics were not measured.
**Project context:** Nav Media Hub is building one public creative-production page, with an inquiry form in the hero, selected approved work directly after it, and a separate private lead inbox. See REQ-001, REQ-005, SRC-REFERENCE-AUDIT and SRC-COMPETITORS.

## Cross-site notes

**Observed:** All four homepages present an identifiable creative offer and a route toward work samples or contact. AEZ foregrounds real-estate listing services; DBE emphasizes short vertical videos and a project CTA; Angle presents service categories and filterable projects; Rafhin foregrounds automotive work and selected projects. These are descriptions of the pages as observed, not claims about business performance.

**Design proposal for Nav Media Hub (not a new requirement):** retain the already approved order from REQ-001 and the design blueprint: header → hero with the Nav inquiry form → selected Nav work → confirmed services/process/proof → footer. Borrow only the general principles of a legible offer, labeled work, and a short inquiry path. The one-page hero form is a project requirement, not a claim that competitor evidence proves higher conversion.

**Do not infer from these references:** that Nav offers each competitor's services; serves the GTA/Ontario or any particular region; has permission to show competitor/client assets; has matching prices, response times, clients or outcomes; or should add ordering, booking, filters, packages, testimonials, and other visible features. Follow workspace/REFERENCE_POLICY.md and project AGENTS.md.
