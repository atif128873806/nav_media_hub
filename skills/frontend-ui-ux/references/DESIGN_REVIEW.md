# Design review

- Can users identify the main task and action without reading every element?
- Does the layout and content fit the business, brand, and approved reference ideas?
- Are spacing, typography, surfaces, borders, and colors consistent through tokens?
- Are functional icons understandable, labeled, and free of decorative AI motifs?
- Do long names, realistic records, missing images, and empty data work?
- Do keyboard focus, labels, error messages, dialogs, and reduced motion work?
- Do mobile tables/forms/navigation remain usable without hiding necessary actions?
- Are primary, secondary, disabled, loading, and destructive actions clear?
- Are assets/copy/metrics real or explicitly labeled fixtures?
- Did browser inspection actually occur? Record pages/viewports and unresolved gaps.
