# Evidence-based Research

Define the question and constraints from project docs. Prefer official documentation,
primary repositories, and authoritative sources for technical behavior. Read sources
rather than inferring architecture or maintenance from search snippets.

For repositories, inspect actual architecture, license, dependencies, tests, history,
and fit before recommending reuse. Studying a repo does not imply cloning it. Verify
current/version-sensitive facts with available connected tools. If browsing is unavailable,
state the limit; do not invent findings or citations.

For design references, follow REFERENCE_POLICY.md. Compare selected relevant patterns and
their business value, not every feature. Record URL/path, observation date, evidence,
inference, tradeoff, and proposed decision separately. Keep recommendations proportional
to user constraints and scope. Save only findings that future tasks need with provenance.
